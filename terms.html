3. What TripLens does and doesn't do

TripLens only reads. It looks for bookings in your email and shows them, with suggestions and guides. It does not make, change or cancel bookings, does not buy or pay for anything, and does not send, change or delete email. Bookings exist only with the airlines, hotels, restaurants and other providers named in your emails.

4. Check before you travel

Trip details are extracted automatically, partly by an AI model on your phone, and can be wrong or incomplete. Day plans, suggestions, place descriptions and spoken guides are generated and may contain mistakes. Distances are straight-line estimates, and "leave by" times are estimates. Always confirm times, places, documents and requirements with your airline, rail operator, hotel or venue. Don't use the Navigator while driving, and keep an eye on your surroundings while walking.
