TripLens

Your trips, found in your inbox. A prototype Android app.

TripLens reads the booking confirmations already in your email (flights, trains, hotels, restaurant tables, tickets and payment receipts) and turns them into:

  • A travel passport with a stamp for every trip and a boarding pass for the next one.
  • Day-by-day journeys that keep your bookings fixed and fill the gaps with things you'd enjoy.
  • A walking guide that points you to what's around you and tells you the story of what's in front of you.

Private by design

Email is read on your phone, by built-in templates and an AI model that runs on the device. TripLens has no server, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Gmail access is read-only and can be revoked at any time.

Status

TripLens is a prototype under test and is not yet available to the public.
